## Full description (4000 chars max)

**IlmAI — علم + AI | Your Complete Islamic Companion**

IlmAI brings together everything a Muslim needs for daily practice and deeper Islamic knowledge — beautifully designed, works offline, and powered by AI.

---

🕌 **QURAN**
• Full Quran with Arabic text, transliteration, and multiple translations
• Word-by-word breakdown for deeper understanding
• Tajweed-colour Mushaf reading mode
• 5 world-renowned reciters (Mishary Rashid, Abdul Basit, Al-Hudhaify, Al-Sudais, Al-Shatri)
• Ayah bookmarks, comprehension tracking, and Tafsir (Ibn Kathir) per verse
• Resume exactly where you left off

🕐 **PRAYER TIMES**
• Auto-detects your location for accurate daily prayer times
• Countdown timer to the next prayer
• Individual per-prayer notification reminders
• Qibla compass using your device sensor
• Multiple calculation methods (Hanafi, Shafi'i, MWL, ISNA, and more)

📖 **HADITH COLLECTIONS**
• Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, 40 Hadith Nawawi, Hadith Qudsi, and more
• Browse by collection or search across all hadith
• Bookmark and save favourite narrations

🤲 **DUA LIBRARY**
• Morning & Evening Adhkar
• Travel, meal, sleep, and daily occasion duas
• Full Arabic with transliteration and translation

✨ **99 NAMES OF ALLAH**
• All 99 Beautiful Names with meanings, explanation, and benefits
• Category filter: Jamal (Beauty), Jalal (Majesty), Kamal (Perfection)
• Share any name as a beautiful text card

🤖 **AI ISLAMIC ASSISTANT**
• Ask any Islamic question in plain language
• Get answers grounded in Quran and Sunnah
• Available in English, Urdu, and Arabic

📚 **ISLAMIC LIBRARY**
• Browse and read classic Islamic books
• Built-in reader — no external apps needed

📊 **STREAK & PROGRESS**
• Daily reading streak with 10-week heatmap calendar
• Track comprehension per ayah (Understood / Partially / Not yet)
• Reading progress saved across sessions

---

**Why IlmAI?**
✅ No account required — everything is local on your device
✅ Works offline after first load (Download Quran in Settings)
✅ Dark mode — easy on the eyes for night reading
✅ Clean, distraction-free design
✅ No ads, no tracking, no data collection

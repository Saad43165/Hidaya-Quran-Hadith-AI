# Privacy Policy — IlmAI

**Last updated: August 2, 2026**

IlmAI ("we", "our", or "the app") is a free Islamic companion app providing Quran reading, Hadith, prayer times, Duas, and AI-assisted learning. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is handled.

---

## 1. Data we collect

### Location (optional)
We request your device location **only** to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your city. Location is:
- Used in real time and never stored on our servers
- Never shared with third parties
- You may deny location permission; the app falls back to a default city (Karachi, Pakistan)

### Local storage only
All user data — bookmarks, reading progress, comprehension notes, vocabulary words, streak history, and settings — is stored **exclusively on your device** using SQLite and AsyncStorage. We have no backend server and never transmit this data anywhere.

### Prayer notifications
If you enable prayer reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. No notification data leaves your device.

### Audio playback
Quran audio is streamed from public, open-access CDNs (everyayah.com). No account or personal data is required or sent.

### AI Assistant
The in-app assistant sends your typed question to the Anthropic Claude API to generate a response. Only the text of your question is sent — no location, identity, or other personal data is attached. Anthropic's privacy policy applies to that request: https://www.anthropic.com/privacy

---

## 2. Data we do NOT collect

- Name, email, or any account information (no login required)
- Device identifiers or advertising IDs
- Crash reports or analytics
- Payment information

IlmAI does not knowingly collect data from children under 13. The app contains no advertising, no in-app purchases, and no social features.
